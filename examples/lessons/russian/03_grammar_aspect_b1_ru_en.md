# Verbal aspect — process vs. result

## Summary

Almost every Russian verb comes as an **aspect pair**: an **imperfective**
(process, repetition, ongoing) and a **perfective** (a single completed action
with a result). Choosing the wrong one is grammatical but changes the meaning —
this is the feature that most shapes how Russians think about events. This unit
drills the contrast across the past and future.

## The core contrast

| imperfective (process) | perfective (result) | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| читать _(chitát')_ | прочитать _(prochitát')_ | to read |
| писать _(pisát')_ | написать _(napisát')_ | to write |
| делать _(délat')_ | сделать _(sdélat')_ | to do / make |
| покупать _(pokupát')_ | купить _(kupít')_ | to buy |
| говорить _(govorít')_ | сказать _(skazát')_ | to say / speak _(suppletive pair)_ |

## Past tense

| Russian | aspect | English |
|---|---|---|
| Я **читал** книгу. | impf. | I was reading / used to read a book. _(process — maybe unfinished)_ |
| Я **прочитал** книгу. | perf. | I read the book. _(finished it — result)_ |
| Она **писала** письмо весь вечер. | impf. | She was writing a letter all evening. |
| Она **написала** письмо. | perf. | She wrote (and finished) the letter. |

## Future tense — the shape changes

This is where aspect is easiest to see, because the two aspects form the future
**differently**:

| Russian | aspect | form | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| Я **буду писать** письмо. | impf. | `буду` + infinitive | I will be writing a letter. |
| Я **напишу** письмо. | perf. | conjugated perfective | I will write (finish) the letter. |

Imperfective future = `буду` + imperfective infinitive. Perfective has **no
`буду`** — the present-looking conjugation of a perfective verb _is_ its future.

## Grammar focus

- **Perfective = boundary reached** — completion, a single whole event, a
  result. Use it for "and then X happened".
    - _evidence:_ Я **прочитал** книгу _(and finished it)_.
- **Imperfective = no boundary** — process, duration, habit, repetition, or a
  general statement of the action.
    - _evidence:_ Я **читал** каждый вечер _(every evening — habit)_.
- **Perfective has no present tense** — a "present" perfective form is future in
  meaning. Only imperfectives describe what's happening _right now_.
    - _evidence:_ Я **пишу** _(impf., now)_ vs. Я **напишу** _(perf., will finish)_.

## Exercises

1. **[multiple_choice]** "I read the whole book yesterday (finished it)." Choose:
    - _choices:_ читал, прочитал, буду читать, читаю
    - _expected:_ прочитал
2. **[cloze]** Habit, every morning: "Каждое утро я ____ кофе." (buy)
    - _choices:_ покупаю, куплю
    - _expected:_ покупаю
3. **[translate_a_to_b]** "I will write the letter (and finish it)."
    - _expected:_ Я напишу письмо.
4. **[morphology_parse]** Identify aspect and tense: "Она будет читать."
    - _expected:_ imperfective, future (`будет` + infinitive) — "she will be reading".

## Review cards

- to read: impf. / perf. → читать / прочитать
- to buy: impf. / perf. → покупать / купить
- I will write (and finish) → я напишу (perf. future)
- I will be writing → я буду писать (impf. future)
