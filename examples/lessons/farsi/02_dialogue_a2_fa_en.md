# At the café — what are you having?

## Summary

A short, natural café exchange in **colloquial** (محاوره‌ای) Tehran Persian,
paired with its **written/formal** (کتابی) equivalents. Register is a core
Persian skill: the spoken and written forms of the same sentence differ
systematically, and learners need both. This unit is flagged for **second-wave
review** — Assimil-style, the whole dialogue comes back ~25 lessons later, not
just its individual cards.

## Dialogue (colloquial)

<div dir="rtl">

**علی:** سلام، خوبی؟

**سارا:** سلام، مرسی، تو خوبی؟

**علی:** آره. چی می‌خوری؟

**سارا:** یه قهوه می‌خوام. تو چی؟

**علی:** منم همینطور. دو تا قهوه، لطفاً.

</div>

## Register: colloquial ↔ written

The same lines, as you would **write** them (کتابی). Notice the vowel and
ending shifts — this is the single most useful pattern for moving between spoken
and written Persian.

| spoken (محاوره‌ای) | written (کتابی) | note |
|---|---|---|
| <span dir="rtl">می‌خوام</span> _(mikhâm)_ | <span dir="rtl">می‌خواهم</span> _(mikhâham)_ | 1sg of خواستن; spoken drops the ه |
| <span dir="rtl">یه</span> _(ye)_ | <span dir="rtl">یک</span> _(yek)_ | "a / one" |
| <span dir="rtl">منم</span> _(manam)_ | <span dir="rtl">من هم</span> _(man ham)_ | "me too" |
| <span dir="rtl">می‌ری</span> _(miri)_ | <span dir="rtl">می‌روی</span> _(miravi)_ | 2sg "you go" |
| <span dir="rtl">خونه</span> _(khune)_ | <span dir="rtl">خانه</span> _(khâne)_ | "house" — â→u before n is the classic shift |

## Vocabulary

<div dir="rtl">

- **خواستن** _(khâstan)_ — to want (present stem: خواه/خوا)
- **قهوه** _(ghahve)_ — coffee
- **مرسی** _(mersi)_ — thanks _(borrowed from French; colloquial)_
- **همینطور** _(hamintor)_ — likewise, the same
- **لطفاً** _(lotfan)_ — please

</div>

## Grammar focus

- **Register shift is systematic, not random** — final `-am` → `-âm`, `آ (â)` →
  `و (u)` before nasals (خانه→خونه), and dropped glottals. Learn the mapping
  once and it generalizes across the whole verb system.
    - _evidence:_ <span dir="rtl">می‌خواهم ← می‌خوام</span>
- **`یه … می‌خوام` frame** — a productive request pattern: `یه ‹noun› می‌خوام`
  ("I'd like a ‹noun›"). Swap the noun to reuse the whole exchange.
    - _evidence:_ <span dir="rtl">یه قهوه می‌خوام.</span>

## Exercises

1. **[translate_b_to_a]** Rewrite in **written** Persian: <span dir="rtl">یه قهوه می‌خوام.</span>
    - _expected:_ <span dir="rtl">یک قهوه می‌خواهم.</span>
2. **[cloze]** Complete the request: <span dir="rtl">یه چای ــــ .</span>
    - _expected:_ <span dir="rtl">می‌خوام</span>
3. **[speaking_prompt]** Order two teas politely, colloquially.
    - _expected:_ <span dir="rtl">دو تا چای، لطفاً.</span>
