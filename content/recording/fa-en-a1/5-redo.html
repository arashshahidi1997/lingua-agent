Recording 5 · Re-records

10 lines · about 1 minutes · one recording, file name 5-redo

  1. Read only the Persian, at a natural, slightly slow pace. Don't read the numbers or the English.
  2. One clean take per line is enough (say it again if you slip; the last take counts). Pause about 2 seconds after each line (count "one, two" in your head).
  3. A slip? Pause, then read the same line again. I keep the last take.
  4. Green names: for a letter, say its name (alef, be…); in a dialogue, it's who speaks, so you can act it out a little.
  5. A quiet room, phone about a hand's width from your mouth. Any recorder app or a Telegram voice note works.
